## Logline
After her last bus is cancelled, a young woman takes a near-empty night metro. An impossible passenger leads her to a forgotten station, then to a room where an antique mirror steals reflections and condemns its victims to lure the next one.

## Promise to the audience
Fear starts in realistic details (silence, light, drops, grips), grows into visual inconsistencies (glass, signage, the passenger), then reveals an old trap. The last metro window must echo the bedroom mirror. **Explain with images, never with a monologue.**

## Canon decisions (V4 §1)
- **On the metro it's the hooded creature C** (pale worn robe, red eyes): shots 14, 16, 17, 24, 26, 32b, 38. Older prompts putting the grey-t-shirt young man in the carriage are **void**. Never use M's reference for C.
- **The young man M** (short hair, grey t-shirt) is a past victim, flashback only: shots 29a, 29b, 30. No hood, no red eyes.
- **Heroine H:** young Black woman, voluminous curly hair, camel coat, dark bag — strict continuity over all shots.
- **Reflection rule:** C is visible behind her *only in the mirror* at 24, then outside the mirror in the flash 32b. The autonomous hand at 31 belongs to *her own* reflection.
- **No new visible characters in inserts:** anomalies come from sets, props, light, reflections.
- **No gore**: the mirror shatter (33) is stylised.
- **14/16/17 camera lock:** same axis, focal length, geometry and pose of C — only its seat changes between cuts.
