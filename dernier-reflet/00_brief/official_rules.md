## Event
- Paris, **Sat 31 Oct → Sun 1 Nov 2026**, 24-hour hackathon, 80 AI creatives.
- Teams of **1 to 3**. Organised by Le Motif and roland.builds.
- Prize: cash, tools, and access to Paris Creator Week 2026 (4–5 Nov).

### ⚠️ Track challenge — announced 31/10 (card « THREE MINUTES TO SCARE: SHORT FILM »)
> **Challenge: Infinite Loop**: the last frame cuts back into the first one. Plus at least **one character speaking on screen with lip sync**. Your video has to be **viral for socials**.

| Requirement | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Last frame cuts back into the first | **39** = push into the black carriage window; the reflection becomes the exact first frame of **01** (Paris aerial) → seamless loop, sound loops too (three pulses → earbud music restarts) |
| On-screen lip sync | **06d** la Vieille warns the heroine face to camera: « Le dernier métro ne ramène personne, ma fille. » · bonus **37** the heroine in the window: « Aidez-moi… » |
| Viral for socials | 2.39:1 master for the jury + **9:16 vertical cut** (`delivery/social_9x16.md`): hook in the first second, burnt-in FR/EN subtitles, loop intact |

## Contribution rules (Notion workspace)
- Every full prompt goes in a code block so it can be copied.
- Use `{{variables}}` for the parts to customise.
- Link every resource to the prompt that generated it (Bibliothèque de ressources → *Prompt source*).
- **Only prompts with status *Approuvé* may be used in final deliverables.** Today both our prompt pages are *Brouillon* → flip them to *Approuvé* in Notion once validated.
- File naming: **`YYYY-MM-DD_sujet_v1`** (e.g. `2026-10-31_shot14-creature_v1.png`).
- Tag every prompt/resource with the track: *Three Minutes to Scare*.
