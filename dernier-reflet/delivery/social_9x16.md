# Vertical cut 9:16 — « viral for socials »

The jury master stays **2.39:1** (Marty's look). The social version is cut from the **same takes**: no extra generation, except optional 9:16 Seedance re-renders of the 2–3 hero close-ups if the punch-ins look soft.

## Format
- 1080×1920, 25 fps, same 180 s edit and **the same loop** (last frame → first frame), so TikTok/Reels autoplay loops seamlessly.
- **Wide shots:** the 2.39 picture sits in the middle of the 9:16 frame, with black above and below. Hook title on top (white, sans serif), burnt-in subtitles below.
- **Close-ups punched in to full height:** 06b (la Vieille speaking), 06c, 12, 13, 15, 17, 26, 32a, 33, 37 (« Aidez-moi… »). Every prompt for these shots asks for the face **in the centre third**, so a 9:16 crop of the 16:9 take keeps the eyes and mouth.
- **Subtitles** FR + EN burnt in: 06b « Le dernier métro ne ramène personne, ma fille. » / *"The last metro brings no one back, child."* · 32a « Tu peux prendre sa place, maintenant. » / *"You can take her place now."* · 37 « Aidez-moi… » / *"Help me…"*

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
The loop means the film has no "start", so the hook is text plus sound on top of shot 01:
- On-screen title 0–2 s: **« Ne prends jamais le dernier métro. »** / *"Never take the last metro."* It echoes la Vieille's line.
- The cold ambient sound of the window (end of 39) carries under 01, then the earbud music kicks in. The viewer feels they arrived mid-loop.
